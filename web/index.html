Prove you solved it

Fill the grid, then make a zero-knowledge proof (PLONK over BLS12-381) that you know a solution. The proof shows that the grid is solved without showing how.


Proof

The prover is Rust compiled to WebAssembly; the verifier is a separate TypeScript implementation. The circuit comes from the Veil language compiler, and the setup is the Ethereum KZG ceremony's (its first 2054 powers). Nothing leaves this page.
